How it works

A quick guide to each part of the app.

The weekly plan
  • Mon and Thu: AI presenter video, posted to all 5 accounts.
  • Tue: carousel one week, image post the next (Facebook and Instagram).
  • Wed: voiceover story about a peptide or supplement from the Story Topics tab.
  • Fri: Friday Fun: a joke, fun fact or light health tip.
  • Sat and Sun: off. Post anything you like from New post.
  • Drafts arrive around 7:15 AM by text. Approve by 10 AM; it posts at 12 PM on the next free day (one post per day).
Approve
  • Each card is one draft. Read it, fix any wording, then tap Approve or Skip.
  • Videos and voiceovers: edit the words in the box. Keep it under 130 words (about 45 seconds).
  • Image posts and carousels: edit each slide. The counters turn red when a line is too long.
  • Film It: tap Watch the original. Ums and long pauses are removed automatically. Each suggested cut is ticked; leave it ticked to cut it, untick to keep it.
  • Open Caption to edit the post text. Open Review notes for the sources and flags.
  • Wellness devices (shockwave, EMS chair, MyoVive, cryoslimming, red light): only how it works, never pain relief, healing or results.
Videos
  • Finished videos and images appear here to check before they post.
  • Looks good marks it reviewed. Don't post stops it; if it's already scheduled, also delete it in GHL Social Planner.
  • In production lists what is still being made. A voiceover or Film It video takes about 45 minutes.
  • After an AI video posts, turn on the AI label on TikTok and YouTube.
Posts
  • Your library of every finished video and image.
  • Upcoming shows what is scheduled, Posted what has gone out, All everything.
  • Filter by videos or images and by service, or search the hook or caption.
  • Open to watch, save or share. Copy caption to repost somewhere else.
  • "Temporary link" means it was never posted; download it if you want to keep it.
Library (B-roll)
  • Your own photos and clips, from the GHL Media Library folder "B-Roll Library" (one subfolder per service).
  • Add B-roll: pick the service, type a few keywords, choose clips or photos, and tap Upload. They are ready to use right away (patient items need the authorization box ticked).
  • Upload new files to that folder in GHL, then tap Sync here (it also syncs every morning).
  • New items need review: set Use in videos, People shown and Consent, add a short description of the body area, then Save.
  • Device footage with no people can be approved in bulk. GLP-1 and any patient photos are reviewed one by one, and patients need a signed authorization.
New post
  • AI video: an AI presenter explains your topic.
  • Voiceover: a narrated science story with animated visuals, great for peptides and supplements.
  • My video (Film It): upload your own recording; it gets trimmed, captioned and given B-roll.
  • Image post or Carousel: one slide, or 3 to 5 swipeable slides (Facebook and Instagram).
  • Pick the service and audience, add keywords (press Enter after each), and paste any article or post you liked.
  • When to post: Next free day (12 PM), As soon as it's ready, or a set time. You get a text when the draft is ready to approve.
Film It: recording tips
  • Hold the phone upright, at eye level, with the light in front of you.
  • Record at 1080p, not 4K, and keep it to about 1 to 2 minutes. Upload on Wi-Fi and keep the screen open.
  • Don't worry about ums or restarts: if you flub a line, pause and say it again. The editor keeps the last good take.
  • Stick to how things work. Avoid promising results; the editor flags those lines as suggested cuts.
  • Only staff on camera, or patients who signed a HIPAA marketing authorization.
If something goes wrong
  • Nothing to approve: tap refresh (top right). Drafts arrive about 3 to 5 minutes after a request.
  • A video is stuck in production: it usually finishes on the next 10 AM run.
  • Signed out: sign in again with your email and password.
  • Everything is also in the Content Hub sheet, tab Content Drafts.
